What it does

Like a paper planner, it keeps today's to-dos and your long-term plans in one place. The difference is an AI assistant that reads the planner with you. It reads what you wrote and your calendar events, and leaves a sticky note about deadlines and overlaps that are easy to miss. You always decide what to do.

What you write

  • Today's to-dos and a one-line diary
  • To-dos sorted A, B, C, D by urgency and importance
  • Plans with a goal and a due date
  • Notes that belong to no date

What the AI assistant tracks

  • Reminders before a deadline
  • Suggestions to group scattered to-dos into a plan
  • Plan drafts and a weekly review
  • Turning a photo of paper notes into to-dos

What you connect

  • Calendar: today's events and free time
  • Your own AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT)
  • iPhone widgets and Lock Screen
  • Sync across devices

Your calendar and your data

If you connect a calendar, DUAL Planner reads the title, date and time of your events. It uses them only to:

  • show your events next to your to-do list on the Today page;
  • find free time between events and suggest it for a to-do you have already written;
  • show, in the weekly review, where the week's hours went.

It never creates, edits or deletes events. Calendar data is not used for advertising, is not sold or shared, and is not used to train AI models. When you disconnect the calendar or delete your account, the stored events are deleted too.
